## About

- I built **[Saient](https://saientai.xyz/)** end to end: an AI agent platform where agents work 24/7 like employees for clinics and small businesses, on phone, WhatsApp, SMS, email and web chat, with one shared memory and people in control. Live with business clients.
- Co-founded **Verly AI** (Jan to Aug 2026), an AI customer support platform across voice, WhatsApp and web chat. I designed the architecture and built the core voice and chat engines.
- Worked at **Nester Labs** as an AI Engineer Intern on voice-agent evals, AI red-teaming and production voice agents. Received a full-time offer.
- B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering at **IIIT Nagpur**.
- Certified in **[Claude Code in Action](https://verify.skilljar.com/c/v2thqwhsrnoj)** by Anthropic.
